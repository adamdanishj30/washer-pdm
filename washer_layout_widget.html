✓ Best Mount: Top-Rear Steel Sheet (Upper Casing)

Directly above the drum suspension springs. Transmits structural vibration and unbalance harmonics with zero dampening. Protected from weather overhang.

✓ Alternative: Upper Rear Access Panel

Directly covers the rear tub bearings and drive pulley. Extremely sensitive to bearing wear and belt noise.

✕ Avoid: Front AI Dial, Drawer & Door Glass

Plastic fascia flexes on rubber clips and dampens high-frequency harmonic signals. Do not mount on front surfaces.
