DIY Wire Splicing Guide

Step 1 Cut Off Male Pins

1/4
Action:

Use your scissors to snip off the black rectangular male plugs from two wires. Discard the black plugs—you only keep the wires.

Why strip the plastic jacket?

Plastic is an electrical insulator. If plastic covers the wires, electricity cannot cross. Stripping reveals the raw copper so the metal can conduct.

Metal-to-Metal Contact:

Twist the two exposed copper ends tightly together like a spiral braid. Once copper touches copper, the electrical circuit is complete!

Safety Achieved!

The tape becomes the new protective plastic jacket! Since all bare copper is sealed inside the tape, wires can touch each other on your desk with zero risk of short circuits.

🎉 Result: You now have a complete, safe Female-to-Female jumper wire!
