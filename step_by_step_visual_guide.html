Step 1 Seat ESP32 on Expansion Shield

1/4
Check Orientation Before Pressing:
  • Orient the ESP32 so its Micro-USB port faces the same outer edge as the power jack on the shield.
  • Look at the pin labels: Align VIN and GND on the ESP32 with VIN / GND on the shield.
  • Press firmly with both thumbs until all 30 pins are fully seated into the black socket headers.
